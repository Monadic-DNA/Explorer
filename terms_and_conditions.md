## 4. Description of Service

Through Monadic DNA Explorer, users can:
- Upload genotype data (e.g., from 23andMe or AncestryDNA).
- Explore published GWAS data curated by the **NHGRI-EBI GWAS Catalog**.
- Apply research filters and view quality scores for each study.
- Optionally use **AI-based interpretation**, chat, and reports. By default these run on **Nillion’s nilAI**, which processes requests inside **Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs)**. You may instead choose another AI provider (Section 8.1).
- Optionally save your data **encrypted on your own device** so you can return to it later.
- Optionally sign in and purchase paid features, such as a subscription or one-time reports.

Uploaded genetic data is processed **locally in your browser**. We **do not store or retain** your genetic data or results on our servers.

## 7. User Data and Ownership

### 7.1 Processing
Uploaded data is processed **locally** in your browser. We do **not** store, back up, or retain your genetic data or analysis results on our servers.

By default, your genetic data and analysis results (matched SNPs and calculated risk scores) are kept **in memory only** and are deleted when you close your browser tab or reload the page.

### 7.2 Saving on Your Device
If you click **Save**, your genetic data, analysis results, and personalization details are encrypted with a passphrase you choose and stored in your browser on that device only. You are responsible for:
- Keeping your passphrase safe. We cannot recover your passphrase or your saved data if you forget it.
- The security of the device and browser where you save data. Anyone who knows your passphrase and can use that browser can open your saved data.
- Deleting saved data when you no longer need it, using the My Data menu or by clearing your browser's site data.

You may also export your results to a file on your device. Exported files are not encrypted. We recommend that you:
- Store exported files securely on encrypted storage
- Delete exported files when no longer needed
- Never upload exported files to cloud services or unsecured locations
